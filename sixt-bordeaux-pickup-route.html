③ 官方取車／還車指示（原文重點）

搭火車抵達：Once arrived at the train station on the platform, please follow the exit "Hall 3 Belcier". Take the ramp up to the ground floor. The SIXT branch is facing the lift in the middle of the hall.
停車場／還車：The car park is located in the Indigo car park P4 rue d'Armagnac/pont du Guit. Take the entrance P4, and go up to 4th floor, you will find a barrier, insert your entrance ticket and the barrier will go up. SIXT parking spots are at 4th floor. Please park your SIXT vehicle imperatively on SIXT reserved spots (written on the floor). Please give the key back to the branch in the train station (Hall 3). If the car park is full, please ring the bell.

④ 攞車前 5 件事

  • 📄 文件齊：國際車牌（IDP）正本、香港正式車牌正本、護照、信用卡（唔收 prepaid card／現金）
  • ⏱ 預鬆時間：旺季排隊 20–30 分鐘屬正常，尤其火車站一帶有工程
  • 📸 影低車身四周（四角＋車頂＋油錶里程）——還車拗數嘅唯一保障
  • ⛽ 燃油政策：Full-to-full —— 攞車滿油，還車前加滿
  • 🚗 車款：4 個 28 吋行李 → 要 PFAR（Premium SUV，如 Kodiaq／Tiguan Allspace） 或 Estate（Passat／Superb）；小型 SUV（3008／T-Roc）唔夠位
💡 「or similar」＝同級派車，唔保證指定型號。取車時可以問一句：「Any chance of the Kodiaq?」

⑤ 還車安排（提醒）

  • 🏠 同站還車：泊返 Indigo P4 4 樓 SIXT 車位 → 鑰匙交返火車站 Hall 3 櫃檯
  • ✈️ 機場還車（Bordeaux Airport, Parking de Location, Avenue René Cassin, Mérignac 33700）
⚠️ 異地還車費用要留意：sixt.com 波爾多頁面寫「one way between stations… a fee may apply（可能收費）」。 如果你係「火車站取車 → 機場還車」，取車時當面同職員 confirm 一次收費，唔好只靠網上舊資料。
